10
. Module 
7
 — Three-Tier Fit and Recommendation Engine
Turn academic and preference data into an understandable application strategy.
Steps
Read the validated student profile.
Compare academic credentials with historical or defined admission benchmarks.
Consider destination, department, course, and financial alignment.
Place institutions into Safe, Best-Fit, or Aspirational categories.
Show the reason for the category and the evidence used.
Allow counsel
l
or review before final shortlist decisions.
Fit categories
• Safe: above 95% admission chance.
• Best-Fit: 50% to 95%.
• Aspirational: below 50% or a reach target.
Supporting analysis
Use historical cohort comparisons, academic scatter-plot context, popular course information, and student-city or geographic diversity insights where available.
Outputs
Balanced application list, fit explanation, counsel
l
or review point, and alternative college recommendations.

. Module 
8
 — Scholarship Discovery and Eligibility
Find funding opportunities that match a student’s academic, demographic, destination, and study preferences.
Steps
Read the student profile and selected department.
Search the scholarship database.
Filter by country, department, gender, study level, eligibility, and deadline.
Open the scholarship detail page.
Review award value, eligibility, academic cutoff, age or demographic rules, required documents, deadline, and official provider.
Save the opportunity and open the verified application portal.
Outputs
Scholarship shortlist, eligibility checklist, document checklist, deadline reminders, and official application link.

. End-to-End Operational Workflow
Eight phases
• Phase 1: onboarding and profile intake.
• Phase 2: preference and academic portfolio.
• Phase 3: recommendations and fit review.
• Phase 4: institution research and shortlist.
• Phase 5: fees and scholarships.
• Phase 6: exam strategy and mock testing.
• Phase 7: assessment and career interpretation.
• Phase 8: counsel
l
or appointment and application action plan.
Decision loop
If evidence is incomplete, the counsel
l
or returns the student to profile updates, additional research, another assessment, or budget refinement before finalizing the action plan.

. Reports, History, and Governance
Reports
Maintain profile history, recommendation decisions, scholarship and fee checks, exam status, assessment results, appointments, counsel
l
or notes, and shortlist changes.
Freshness controls
Audit scholarships and deadlines, refresh currency or cost references, verify official links, and flag expired or outdated records.
Governance
Restrict access by role, log sensitive changes and downloads, preserve assessment versions, and provide authorized exports for operational planning.
